Privacy Policy for Health Tracker

1. Introduction

Welcome to Health Tracker ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application connected to the WHOOP API.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information from your WHOOP account through the WHOOP API, with your consent. This may include:

  • Profile information (e.g., name, email address)
  • Physical attributes (e.g., height, weight, gender)
  • Activity and performance data
  • Recovery and sleep data
  • Strain and workout data

Please note that the data you share via WHOOP's API is also subject to WHOOP's privacy practices, which we recommend reviewing.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our service
  • Analyse your fitness and wellness data to offer insights and recommendations
  • Improve and optimise our application
  • Respond to your requests or questions

4. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. By using our service, you acknowledge the inherent risks in online data transmission.

We retain your data for a period of 24 months after your last interaction with our service, unless a longer retention period is required by law. After this period, your data will be securely deleted. You may request deletion of your data sooner by contacting us.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following situations:

  • With your consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property

6. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Where possible, we limit the transfer of your data outside of your home jurisdiction. If your data is transferred outside the UK or European Economic Area (EEA), we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, such as using services in countries deemed to provide adequate protection by the relevant data protection authorities.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • The right to access, update, or delete your information
  • The right to rectification
  • The right to object to processing (where applicable)
  • The right of restriction (where applicable)
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to withdraw consent

If you are located in the UK or EEA, these rights are protected under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For users in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) may apply. We will work to fulfil these requests where feasible, but please note that certain rights may be limited by technical constraints.

8. Third-Party Services

Our application may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies to understand how your data is handled. If you have concerns about third-party data practices, we recommend managing your connected services via their respective privacy settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. For material changes, we will notify you by posting the updated Privacy Policy on this page. In non-material cases or where urgent updates are required to comply with legal obligations, we may provide a shorter notice period. The "Last updated" date will always reflect the latest version.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@matthewman.com
