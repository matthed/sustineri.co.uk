How I work

I build and run technology functions that have to deliver. Strategy, architecture, security, infrastructure, engineering leadership, AI, the full remit.

The common thread in my work is consequence: when the tech is wrong, something real breaks. Customers, regulators, revenue, people. That has shaped how I operate. I stay close to the detail, make the architecture calls myself, and carry decisions through to delivery.

No long exploration phase, no deck of recommendations for someone else to implement. Diagnosis and delivery.

What AI has changed

AI tooling has collapsed the distance between the strategic CTO and the engineers building the product.

I came up through the hands-on years: Java, production systems, things that had to stay up. A leader who came up through code can again carry decisions all the way from architecture into delivery, so I am back in the tech as well as the boardroom.

For the businesses I work with, the job is getting AI out of the proof-of-concept stage and into things customers actually use.
